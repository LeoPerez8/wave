## Implementation

Core (`packages/core/src/bounce.ts`, exported through `index.ts`):

- `BOUNCE_AMOUNT = { min: 0, max: 120, step: 5, default: 100 }`.
- `readBounceAmount(stored)`: null or anything that isn't a plain decimal number (`""`, `"abc"`, `"50%"`, `"NaN"`, `"Infinity"`, `"1e2"`, `"0x40"`, JSON) → 100; numbers off range clamp to 0/120; others snap to the nearest 5%.
- `storedBounceAmount(amount)`: null at 100 (syncs as unset), else the snapped number.
- `bounceScale(amount, reduceMotion)`: 0 under reduce motion at every amount, else amount/100 clamped to 0–1.2.
- `RETURN_SPRINGS`: the four springs above, moved from the components unchanged.
- `bounceSpring(base, scale)`: keeps stiffness and mass; 1 returns `base` exactly; 0 returns at least critical damping with `overshootClamping: true`; otherwise damping is solved so overshoot = base overshoot × scale (strictly monotonic). `bounceOvershoot(spring)` exposes the measure.
- `mixPull(drag, give = 1)`: offset = min(drag, give × rubber band). `progress` and `armed` use only the finger's travel, so `MIX_PULL_THRESHOLD` is invariant.

App:

- `settings/bounceAmount.ts`: `persistedSetting` key `settings.bounceAmount`, `synced: true`, Core reader/writer, 300 ms persist delay (like Glass), registered in `sync/syncedPreferences.ts`.
- `motion/bounce.ts`: `useBounceScale()` (preference + Reanimated `useReducedMotion`) and `useBounceSpring(base)`.
- `PullRefreshRow`, `SwipeableRow`, `MiniPlayer` and `ToastHost` use those values. PullRefreshRow passes them to its worklets through derived shared values. Thresholds, `enabled`/in-flight guards, haptics, TalkBack actions, Undo toasts and loading states are untouched.
- `settings/MotionSettingsSection.tsx` (Settings › Appearance › Motion): "Bounce amount" slider showing e.g. "100%", explanatory note, and "Reset bounce to 100%" (disabled at 100%).
- `docs/spec.md`: Settings page list, Visual design (Bounce amount with affected/unaffected interactions), Swipe actions, Pull for new mixes and What syncs.

No Android device or emulator and no Android SDK in this session, so nothing was built or run on a phone. Still required on the owner's phone with the CI build:

1. Settings › Appearance › Motion: the slider shows the percentage, drags and taps land on 5% steps, Reset bounce to 100% returns it and disables itself; TalkBack announces "Bounce amount, 100%" and swipe up/down step it 5%.

Confirm 100% feels as before, 0% holds the row still while the arrow fades in, turns into the spinner with the tick at the same finger travel and refreshes (Undo toast as usual), 120% gives a bit more with no clipped covers or accidental refreshes, and a pull while a section is updating does nothing.
3. At the same amounts: swipe a song row short and past a third (Add to Queue, Remove with Undo), swipe the mini player short and past the skip threshold, swipe a toast up short and past dismissal. Check the rebound at 0% stops at rest and 120% is only slightly livelier.
4. Vertical scrolling of Home and lists, horizontal scrolling of the rows, taps and long-presses unchanged at every amount.
5. With Android's Remove animations on at 120%: no row give and no rebound, and every action still works.
6. Change the amount on one signed-in phone, check it arrives on another, and that it survives a restart.

# #614 — Separate lyrics Story background and sticker

The owner reported #605's padded sticker and background-colour extras still produced a flat, square, oversized card in Instagram on ti. This follow-up changes only lyrics Stories.

Core's public `lyricStoryFrame` now centres the card within 60% of a 1080 × 1920 Story's width and 75% of its height. A short square card is 648 × 648 at (216, 636); a full-height card is 648 × 1152 at (216, 384). The radius remains 5.5% of the visible card width. The preview and native exporter use this same decision. Palette selection, solid card colour and lyric typography stay as before.

`writeStoryImages` creates two cache PNGs from the standalone card:

- `StoryBackground` draws the complementary top-to-bottom gradient into a 1080 × 1920 image. It is sent as the Stories intent's data URI with MIME `image/png`.
- `StorySticker` draws the solid card into a tight 648 px wide ARGB image, with adaptive height rounded to the nearest pixel and transparent, anti-aliased rounded corners. There is no surrounding padding. It is sent as `interactive_asset_uri`, keeping it on the editable sticker layer.

`shareStoryImages` grants Instagram read access to both files. The gradient and card are separate images; there are no added size or position extras. A failed export rejects before launching Instagram and removes an already-written sticker if background creation fails. Save image, WhatsApp and More keep the original standalone card. Song and Recap keep the existing `shareToInstagramStory` path, including their original background-colour extras.

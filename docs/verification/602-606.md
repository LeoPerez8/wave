## Implemented policy

Both public candidate interfaces, `magicLibraryCandidates` and
`magicCatalogCandidates`, apply the same strict eligibility decision:

- Known styles must match (or be neighbours at Close). If a family profile
  is available, at least half its mass must support the declared style, for
  seed and candidate alike. A minor first style/tag cannot override the
  remainder. The support families come from Core's style/tag table, so Heavy
  can span Rock and Metal and Soft / acoustic can span Rock and Folk.
  Matching known styles remain direct evidence when profiles are absent.
- If either style is unknown, family profiles must be known, their shared
  mass must meet the selected threshold (0.8 Same genres, 0.65 Close), **and**
  the candidate must be close through the seed artist, graph, or YouTube
  seed-radio association. Missing/null/empty family profiles cannot establish
  that fallback. Coarse Reggae can retain compatible reggae neighbours, but
  cannot admit coarse Rock merely through association.
- Balanced and Neighbours retain their existing family/shared-mass rules.
  Library Wander still admits all genres, including unknown ones. YouTube
  Wander still needs known profiles, 0.2 affinity/shared mass, and its existing
  catalog/radio relationship; it has never had Library Wander's full freedom.

Eligibility precedes ranking/picking. Taste, favorites, downloads and session
signals cannot promote an ineligible automatic recommendation. Explicit Play
Next/Add to Queue remains an owner action; its positive session signal does
not authorize other incompatible recommendations. No artist-name exclusion
was added.

The mobile service already calls both public gates for its initial and every
later batch and after session restoration. No separate mobile gate or saved
session migration is needed. Sessions saved before `seedStyle` existed now
use family evidence plus closeness. Existing queued entries are preserved;
this change decides future automatic recommendations, not retrospective
removal of already queued or owner-inserted songs.

`docs/spec.md` updates the Magic Queue policy and the Discover level card's
reference to it. Artist/Genre mixes retain their existing engine policy.

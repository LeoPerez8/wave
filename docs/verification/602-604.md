## Before

Settings › Swipe actions showed the master switch, then, while it was on, a **Swipe right** catalog line followed by all eleven `SWIPE_CHOICES` as radio rows and a **Swipe left** catalog line followed by the same eleven rows (22 radio rows in total), with the Remove / unsupported-action note under the second list. Switched off, both lists disappeared. The small tracked-caps direction labels were dominated by the two long lists (source inspection of `apps/mobile/src/app/settings/swipe-actions.tsx` at `00f99081`; no on-device screenshot was taken).

## Implementation

- `apps/mobile/src/app/settings/swipe-actions/index.tsx` (moved from `swipe-actions.tsx`, so `/settings/swipe-actions` and `wave://settings/swipe-actions` still open it): the switch and its one-line explanation, then two compact rows, **Swipe left** and **Swipe right**.
- `apps/mobile/src/settings/SwipeDirections.tsx`:
  - `SwipeDirectionRow`: Tabler `IconSwipeLeft` / `IconSwipeRight` glyph, the direction as the row title, the chosen action as its second line. At least 56dp (`grid.row`) tall with 8dp vertical padding, so it grows with text instead of clipping. `accessibilityRole="button"`, label "Swipe left, Remove", hint "Chooses what swiping left runs". With swipes off the row stays (so the page still says what each direction would run) but is dimmed (title in `secondary`, glyph in `tertiary`, per DESIGN.md's Pewter Is Not Text rule), `disabled`, and `accessibilityState={{ disabled: true }}`; the line under the switch says song rows don't swipe.
  - `SwipeChoicePickerScreen`: a Settings page titled with the direction (`SettingsPageScreen`, which now also takes a `title`), inside its `ScrollView`, so every choice is reachable at any font scale. A radiogroup named for the direction, one `ChoiceRow` per `SWIPE_CHOICES` entry: `accessibilityRole="radio"`, `accessibilityState={{ checked }}`, a check glyph on the current one (not colour alone), and a label carrying the direction ("Remove, swipe left"; `ChoiceRow` gained an optional `accessibilityLabel`). The Remove / unsupported-action note is the footnote under the choices, where the choice is made, instead of on the main page.
  - A tap calls `setSwipeChoice(side, choice)` (which writes only that field over the current settings) and goes back (`router.back()`, or `router.replace("/settings/swipe-actions")` when the picker was opened from a link with nothing under it). Back from the header or the system leaves the setting untouched, since nothing is written until a choice is tapped.
- `apps/mobile/src/app/settings/swipe-actions/left.tsx`, `right.tsx`: the two picker routes.
- Unchanged: `settings.swipeActions` storage, Core's `readSwipeSettings` / `storedSwipeSettings` and defaults (on, right Add to Queue, left Remove), Wave account sync (`persistedSetting` with `synced: true`), the Settings row's "Off" summary, `rowSwipeActions` and every row's swipe availability. No Core logic changed: the new code is presentation and navigation only.
- `docs/spec.md`, Swipe actions: describes the compact rows, the pickers, their accessibility, the footnote's place and the off state.

## Environment and unverified acceptance evidence

No Android device or emulator was available to this session, and the APK was not built. Nothing here was rendered, so the following acceptance items remain open and need a device with a build carrying this branch:

1. Before/after screenshots of Settings › Swipe actions (on and off) and of both pickers, in light and dark themes.
2. Text at 1x, 1.3x and 2x: rows grow without clipping; each picker scrolls to Remove and to the footnote at 2x.
3. A narrow width (for example 320dp or split screen): titles, action names and the footnote wrap rather than truncate.
4. Touch targets: each direction row and choice row at least 48dp tall (by construction 56dp minimum).
5. TalkBack: a direction row reads as "Swipe left, Remove, button" with its hint, and as disabled with swipes off; a picker reads its title as a heading and each choice as "<action>, swipe left, radio button, checked/not checked".
6. Behaviour: choosing an action changes only that direction, returns to Swipe actions showing it, and a song row's swipe runs it; system Back and the header's Back from a picker leave both directions as they were; a change syncs to a second phone through the Wave account; switching off dims both rows, blocks opening the pickers and stops rows swiping; switching back on restores the kept choices.
7. Predictive Back on the picker behaves as on other Settings sub-pages.

## Optional further design ideas (not acceptance requirements)

- Show a tiny preview of the uncovered action (the swipe's icon and teal or red label, as the row draws it) beside each choice, so the picker shows what the swipe will look like.
- Let each direction be set to "Nothing", leaving the other one active, instead of only the all-or-nothing switch.
- Mark, in the picker, the choices that only some rows offer (Download, Go to Album, Fix Song) with a short secondary line.
- Offer a "Reset to defaults" text button under the direction rows, as Glass does, once more than a few settings live on this page.

The first numbered version of Wave, a local music player for Android.

- Plays the music files on your phone gaplessly, with the notification, lock screen, Bluetooth and Android Auto controls.
- Library by artist, album, song, genre, playlist and favorites, kept up to date with your music folders.
- Now Playing with synced lyrics, the queue, shuffle, repeat and a sleep timer.
- Search that ignores case and accents, and a Home tab with what you play most and last.
- Lyrics, genres and artist photos found online, each behind its own switch in Settings.
- YouTube Music in Search, Discover and artist pages, and songs downloaded into your Library as tagged files.
- Playlists imported from M3U files or a link, and your BlackPlayer play counts brought over.
- Every play recorded, for listening stats.

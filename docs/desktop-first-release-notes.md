The first version of Wave for the desktop, a music player for Windows and Linux.

- Plays the music files on your computer gaplessly, with crossfade, the system media controls and a tray icon.
- Library by artist, album, song, genre, playlist and favorites, kept up to date with your music folders.
- A player bar, synced lyrics and the queue in a side panel, shuffle, repeat and a sleep timer.
- Search that ignores case and accents, a Home tab with what you play most and last, and Discover.
- Playlists, imported from M3U files or a link.
- YouTube Music in Search, Discover and artist pages, and songs downloaded into your Library as tagged files.
- Your Wave account, so this computer joins your phone as one more device.
